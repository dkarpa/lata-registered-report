# Is Algorithm Aversion a Rule or a Weight?

Materials for the Stage 1 Registered Report "Is Algorithm Aversion a Rule or a Weight? Decision Strategies and Reasons Elicited by a Large Language Model", submitted to *Psychological Science*. No data have been collected yet.

The study is a smartphone survey of 4,000 adults in Finland, recruited through the Norstat panel and offered in Finnish, Swedish and English ("Trust in Algorithms in the Age of AI", LATA). Respondents choose six times between two hypothetical automated systems that Kela could use to decide benefit applications. Each attribute stays hidden until the respondent taps it. Afterwards, an LLM interviewer asks how they chose, and a second LLM codes each transcript for decision strategies and reasons.

## Files

- `S1_questionnaire.pdf`: Supplement S1. The English questionnaire, including the consent and context screens, the choice tasks, and the settings and system prompt of the LLM interviewer.
- `S2_pipeline.pdf`: Supplement S2. Technical documentation of the survey platform, the conjoint stimulus, the interaction recorder, the LLM interviewer and the transcript coder.
- `coding_protocol.pdf`: the catalogue of strategies, reasons and aversion mechanisms, the rule for choosing the coding model, the coding prompt, and the instructions for the human coders.
- `LICENSE`: CC0 1.0.
- `CITATION.cff`: citation metadata.

The Qualtrics file, the stimulus code, the Finnish and Swedish versions, the data and the analysis scripts follow with the Stage 2 submission.
